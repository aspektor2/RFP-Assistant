== COMPANY OVERVIEW ==
Assertive Yield (AY) is a global publisher technology company created by publishers, for publishers. Founded in 2019 by Nils Lind and headquartered in The Hague, Netherlands, with commercial teams across North America and Europe. AY has been bootstrapped and independent since founding, achieving 70% year-over-year revenue growth in 2023. The AY Next-Gen Publisher Tech Platform is an all-in-one toolkit for maximizing monetization revenue, pioneering real-time yield revenue management from all revenue sources with exceptionally granular data. AY is revenue-agnostic and committed exclusively to the interests of publishers: clients retain full control over their data and supply chains.

== PRODUCTS ==
- Yield Intelligence: real-time revenue analytics across all revenue sources in one centralized platform. Reporting latency of roughly 30 seconds, analysis across 300+ dimensions and metrics, real-time alerts, revenue predictions with 94-98% accuracy, and streamlined troubleshooting.
- Yield Manager: a powerful hybrid header bidding wrapper (client-side) combining 11+ solutions in one platform, including A/B/C testing, ad refresh, AI Layouts, web ad mediation, and bid request optimization.
- AY Prebid Server: server-side operations management with AI Traffic Shaping, AI Dynamic Flooring, and integrated identity (ID) solutions.
- Yield Optimizer: AI-driven Dynamic Flooring and Traffic Shaping for publishers and SSPs, designed to boost revenue and improve user lifetime value.

== DYNAMIC FLOORING ==
- Real-time, AI-optimized floor pricing that protects inventory from being sold below value.
- Optimized for Prebid, Amazon (A9/TAM), Google Open Bidding, and Google AdX.
- Set and adjust optimal floor prices in GAM, Amazon, and Prebid from one place, with approximately 99% hands-free integration and continuous optimization.

== TRAFFIC SHAPING ==
- AI-powered traffic shaping for publishers and SSPs; machine learning models continuously adapt to traffic patterns.
- Reduces redundant and duplicated bids, surfaces inventory that is otherwise hidden from demand partners, and improves auction accuracy.
- Optimizes over 120 billion impressions per month, giving publishers greater visibility into their supply chain across all inventory tiers.

== DATA & ANALYTICS ==
- A simple script integration collects, normalizes, and cleans every event on the publisher's pages.
- First-party data from all revenue streams is available in real time with highly granular detail across 300+ dimensions and metrics.
- Designed for ad ops, rev ops, and yield teams: slicing and dicing, custom experiments, prebid analytics, and adstack performance monitoring.
